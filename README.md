# 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI Dashboard** built using the Blinkit Grocery Sales Dataset.

The dashboard helps business stakeholders monitor sales performance, analyse customer behaviour, compare outlet performance, identify high-performing product categories, and make data-driven business decisions through interactive visualizations.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Retail companies generate millions of transaction records every year.

Without proper dashboards, identifying sales trends, outlet performance, and customer preferences becomes difficult.

This dashboard solves that problem by converting raw sales data into interactive business intelligence reports.

# 📊 Business Insights

### 📍 Outlet Performance

- Tier 3 outlets generated the highest sales.
- Supermarket Type 1 contributed the maximum revenue.

---

### 🛒 Product Analysis

- Fruits & Vegetables generated the highest sales.
- Snack Foods were among the top-selling categories.

---

### 📦 Outlet Distribution

- Medium-sized outlets contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Different outlet sizes showed distinct sales patterns.

---

### ⭐ Customer Experience

- Average customer rating remained around **3.9**.
- Dashboard enables monitoring of customer satisfaction across categories.
